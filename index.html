About

Char Jones grew up with a Broadway character-actor father and a drama-teacher mother in a big, wonderfully theatrical, sing-at-the-dinner-table kind of family. It’s no surprise that she grew up to pursue storytelling, clowning, and classical acting—although she did briefly detour into the tech world, pursuing other creative passions before eventually pivoting full-time to acting.

Char is based in New York City, where she grew up, with ties to Los Angeles and Chicago. She made her professional debut at the age of eight as Bo Gordon in Off the Map, and her acting career has since taken her through film, television, regional theatre, and Off-Broadway. She holds both a Bachelor’s and Master’s degree in Computer Science from Northwestern University, where she also studied Theatre and Dance. Char is classically trained and has studied with the Royal Shakespeare Company, Juilliard Extension Program, T. Schreiber Studio, Katie Cappiello, UCB, and Prague Shakespeare Company. She is also a trained aerialist, dancer, and diver.

Alongside acting, Char has spent plenty of time on the other side of the curtain. She has worked on The 25th Annual Putnam County Spelling Bee Off-Broadway and The Rocky Horror Show on Broadway, as well as with the Amateur Comedy Club and Theatre for Young Audiences (TYA) programs throughout the Northeast. Earlier in her career, she worked in talent management at IMG Models and supported Ronan Farrow at Ronan Production Group, working on projects across The New Yorker, HBO, and Audible. Having worked both onstage and behind the scenes, Char has a pretty good sense of all the moving parts that go into making a story—and loves being part of the process from wherever she’s needed.
